The problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. Day and month swap: “7 April” goes out as “4 July”. Any channel except a booking-v3 form.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type month first, as told, and the form reads the date day first, the tenant’s way. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants.
  • one-day: the date moves by exactly one day, mostly back: “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday”, “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” A month-first parser swaps day and month; it cannot move a date by one day. This points at time zones. These are the reports the desk already read as one-day moves (desk-notes.json).

How the merge hid them

  • Subjects. Decision 0001 merges on subject alone. The same subjects come in for different problems, so all three landed in one ticket.
  • Intake tags. The tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant: its locale and its booking form. Count by tag and the split is wrong.
  • Repeats. The ticket export comes in overlapping pages, so some reports appear twice. Count one report per id.
  • Footnotes. The kb’s cause table files the one-day moves under parser by tenant and footnotes them. Their bodies describe a different bug.
